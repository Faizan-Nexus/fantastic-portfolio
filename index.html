iCBEEM 2026

Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Presented at iCBEEM 2026, Sukkur IBA University — January 2026

AI for resilient SMEs: Triple Helix framework driving measurable sustainability gains.

Click to read abstract

In Pakistan, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and startups are essential to economic growth and contribute nearly 40% to GDP while creating significant employment opportunities, but they face persistent challenges. The lack of access to capital, business inefficiency, low innovation capacity, and poor data management all hamper competitiveness, while weak connections between academia, industry, and government limit knowledge transfer and the dissemination of innovation. This paper proposes a multi-dimensional theoretical framework that combines the Triple Helix model of academia–industry–government collaboration with Sustainable Development Goal (SDG)-driven entrepreneurship to address these challenges. This approach focuses on knowledge creation, digital transformation, and the application of circular economy principles to enhance efficiency and resource adaptability. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is identified as a major turning point within this paradigm. AI-powered tools and predictive analytics can empower SMEs by improving market forecasting, streamlining operations, and enhancing customer engagement. These technologies optimize resource use, reduce waste, and strengthen financial risk management. Moreover, AI-facilitated platforms bridge the gap between startups and investors, simplify skill development through intelligent learning systems, and guide data-driven policy design. The conclusion of this analysis emphasizes that embedding AI into Pakistan’s entrepreneurial ecosystem can foster sustainable innovation and inclusive growth. By integrating AI-powered strategies with SDG goals, SMEs in Pakistan can become increasingly competitive and resilient. Finally, such incorporation of AI is vital for driving long-term economic progress and international competitiveness in emerging economies.

ICoMET 2026

Evaluating Deep Learning and Hybrid Quantum Algorithms for Lung Disease Classification

Accepted to IEEE Xplore, ICoMET 2026, Sukkur IBA University — May 2026

Vision Transformers and quantum-inspired features for robust multi-class lung diagnosis.

Click to read abstract

The lungs are vital organs of the human body, and lung diseases like Covid-19, pneumonia, and TB are major health concerns. Advancements in AI and deep learning have transformed medical image diagnosis that helps in early detection of disease and diagnosis. This study compares standard deep learning techniques with hybrid quantum models to identify four lung diseases which include TB, COVID-19, Viral Pneumonia and Bacterial Pneumonia. Basic Machine Learning (ML) models like Support Vector Machine (SVM) and advance deep learning architectures such as Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), Vision Transformer (ViT), Residual Neural Network (ResNet 50), Visual Graphic Geometry (VGG-16), Efficient-NetB0 and hybrid architectures such as Hybrid Quantum Convolutional Neural Network (HQCNN) and Hybrid Quantum Support Vector Machine (HQSVM) are employed in this study. The models are trained both from scratch and through transfer learning depending on their respective architecture. Evaluation results indicate that the ViT achieves the best classification performance, while HQSVM demonstrates superior performance against different deep learning models such as Efficient-NetB0 and VGG-16 which demonstrates that hybrid quantum approaches hold promise for medical imaging tasks.
